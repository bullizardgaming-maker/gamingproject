The mark

A coin, an eye and a pair of horns.

Bullizard holds the buyer's money and watches the trade until the goods land. The mark says that in one shape you can draw from memory.

Ring The coin. Every order starts as money held in escrow.
Slit The lizard's pupil, and the coin slot. It watches the trade until the buyer confirms.
Horns The bull. One crescent, wider than the coin, so the mark reads as a guard and not a face.
